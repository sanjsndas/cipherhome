About Us

We write about the spaces
that shape how we live.

Home Geometry is an independent journal exploring interior design, architecture, materials, and the quiet intelligence of well-considered space.

Our Story

Home Geometry began in 2019 as a personal notebook — a place to collect observations about why some rooms feel right and others don't. That curiosity grew into a publication read by design professionals, homeowners, and anyone who cares about the built environment.

We are not a trend site. We don't chase viral kitchens or predict color of the year. Instead, we focus on the enduring principles that make spaces work: proportion, light, material honesty, and the relationship between a building and its context. Our articles are researched, reported, and written with care.

“Design is not decoration. It is the thoughtful arrangement of space, light, and material to support human life.”

Every week, we publish in-depth guides, case studies, and practical knowledge for design-conscious readers. Whether you're renovating a small apartment or simply trying to make your living room feel more intentional, we aim to give you tools and ideas that last longer than a season.

Home Geometry is independently owned and editorially independent. We do not accept sponsored content disguised as editorial. When we recommend a product or material, it's because we believe it's worth your attention — not because someone paid us to say so.

What We Stand For

Clarity over clutter

We believe good design is legible. We write clearly, avoid jargon, and explain the “why” behind every idea so you can apply it in your own space.

Depth over trends

We're more interested in principles that endure than in what's trending on social media. Our archive is built to be useful years from now.

Honesty in materials

We champion materials that age gracefully and express their true nature — stone that looks like stone, wood that feels like wood.

Editorial Independence

Home Geometry is committed to publishing accurate, independent, and useful information. Our editorial team operates separately from any advertising or commercial partnerships.

  • We do not publish sponsored articles or paid product placements.
  • We clearly label any affiliate links and never let them influence our recommendations.
  • We correct errors promptly and transparently.
  • We prioritize the reader's interest above all else.

If you have a question about our editorial process, please contact us. We're always happy to hear from readers.
